# AIRCRAFT ENGINE OIL & LUBRICATION SYSTEM MAINTENANCE MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)
SYSTEM: Engine Oil System (ATA 79 / ATA 72)

SECTION 1: HIGH OIL TEMPERATURE AND GRADUAL OIL PRESSURE LOSS
1. If engine oil temperature increases above normal limits while oil pressure decreases during operation:
- Inspect engine oil reservoir fluid level and perform oil consumption rate calculation per AMM Chapter 79-00-00.
- Check main engine oil filter element and bypass indicator pin; remove and inspect filter cartridge for metallic wear debris or contamination.
- Verify oil pressure relief valve cracking pressure and inspect thermal bypass valve operation in the oil cooler line.
- Test oil temperature transmitter/sensor calibration and wiring harness connector pins for signal drift or corrosion.
- Perform oil cooler airflow duct inspection for blockage or thermostatic valve malfunction.
- Execute engine ground run operational test to monitor oil pressure and temperature stabilization across engine power settings.

SECTION 2: LUBRICATION PUMP & CHIP DETECTOR MAINTENANCE
2. Magnetic chip detector and oil pump inspection:
- Remove and inspect magnetic chip detector plugs for iron or alloy metal particle accumulation.
- If magnetic particles are detected, submit chip sample for laboratory alloy analysis before engine clearance.
