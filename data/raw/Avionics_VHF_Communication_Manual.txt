# AIRCRAFT AVIONICS & VHF COMMUNICATION SYSTEM MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Component & Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM/CMM)
SYSTEM: Avionics & Communications (ATA 23)

SECTION 1: VHF COMMUNICATION SYSTEM INTERMITTENT FAULTS
1. If intermittent communication loss occurs between the Audio Control Panel (ACP) and VHF transceiver:
- Inspect electrical connectors, wiring harness pin retention, and coaxial cable shield grounding per AMM Chapter 23-10-00.
- Perform visual check of VHF antenna mounting base for corrosion, sealant degradation, or loose mounting hardware.
- Test Audio Control Panel (ACP) push-to-talk (PTT) switch continuity and audio transformer output signals.
- Check VHF transceiver line-replaceable unit (LRU) rack seating, pin engagement, and internal BITE error log entries.
- Perform radio transmission quality and VSWR (Voltage Standing Wave Ratio) test across active VHF frequency bands before LRU replacement.

SECTION 2: AUDIO CONTROL PANEL & WIRING HARNESS INTEGRITY
2. Connector and harness inspection before LRU swapping:
- Perform pin-to-pin wiring resistance and insulation isolation tests on communication control harnesses.
- Verify ground bus connection resistance does not exceed 2.5 milliohms.
