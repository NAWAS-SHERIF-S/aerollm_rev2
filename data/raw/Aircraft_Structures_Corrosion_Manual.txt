# AIRCRAFT STRUCTURES & CORROSION CONTROL MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Structural Repair Manual (SRM) / AMM
SYSTEM: Standard Structures & Surface Protection (ATA 51 / ATA 20)

SECTION 1: FUSELAGE SURFACE CORROSION CLEANING & DEPTH ASSESSMENT
1. If localized surface corrosion is identified on fuselage aluminum skin or structural panel areas:
- Clean affected area thoroughly using approved solvent cleaning agents and non-metallic abrasive pads per SRM Chapter 51-20-00.
- Perform detailed visual and optical depth measurement (using depth micrometer or ultrasonic thickness gauge) to quantify metal loss.
- Compare corrosion depth against allowable structural damage limits published in the Structural Repair Manual (SRM).
- Remove surface oxidation completely using fine aluminum oxide abrasives without exceeding structural skin thickness tolerances.
- Apply chemical conversion coating (Alodine / Chromate treatment) followed by epoxy primer and approved topcoat system.
- Schedule follow-up non-destructive inspection (NDI) or eddy current testing if corrosion depth exceeds minor surface thresholds.

SECTION 2: FASTENER AND ACCESS PANEL CORROSION CONTROL
2. Corrosion treatment near access panel fasteners:
- Inspect fastener countersinks and surrounding structural skin for galvanic corrosion or moisture entrapment.
- Re-seal access panel perimeters with approved polysulfide structural sealant before flight authorization.
