# AIRCRAFT LANDING GEAR SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING & MAINTENANCE MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)
SYSTEM: Landing Gear System (ATA 32)

SECTION 1: LANDING GEAR RETRACTION & INDICATION FAULTS
1. If the main landing gear indicates an unsafe condition during retraction or cycling:
- Verify proximity sensor alignment, target clearance gaps (1.5mm - 3.0mm), and wiring harness integrity per AMM Chapter 32-60-00.
- Inspect landing gear door actuator, mechanical uplatch mechanism, and hydraulic extension/retraction lines for physical damage or obstruction.
- Check main landing gear selector valve operation and measure hydraulic supply pressure during gear sweep tests.
- Perform electrical continuity and BITE diagnostic testing on proximity detector switches and cockpit gear indication lights.
- Perform complete ground operational gear retraction test using hydraulic ground power units (GPU) and safety jack stands prior to flight release.

SECTION 2: BRAKE & WHEEL ASSEMBLY INSPECTION
2. Landing gear wheel, brake, and tire maintenance:
- Inspect brake assembly wear indicator pins and hydraulic brake line connections for leakage or pressure loss.
- Examine wheel rim flanges and tire tread condition for excessive wear, cut depth, or thermal degradation.
- Check emergency extension system nitrogen bottle charge pressure and manual gear release mechanical linkages.
