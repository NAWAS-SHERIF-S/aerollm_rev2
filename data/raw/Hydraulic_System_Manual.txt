# AIRCRAFT HYDRAULIC SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING & MAINTENANCE MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Maintenance Manual
SYSTEM: Hydraulic Power (ATA 29)

SECTION 1: HYDRAULIC LIFT & ACTUATOR MALFUNCTION
1. If the hydraulic lift or actuator fails to operate properly or low system pressure is indicated:
- Check hydraulic reservoir fluid level and inspect sight glass for aeration or fluid discoloration.
- Inspect hydraulic lines, fittings, quick disconnects, and valve connections for active fluid leakage.
- Inspect the hydraulic lift actuator cylinder, selector valve assembly, and piston seals for mechanical wear or bypass leakage.
- Measure primary hydraulic system pressure using a calibrated ground pressure test gauge.
- Perform the applicable hydraulic troubleshooting procedure per AMM Chapter 29-10-00.
- Bleed trapped air from the hydraulic system and perform a full operational ground test prior to aircraft release.

SECTION 2: HYDRAULIC PUMP TROUBLESHOOTING
2. Engine-Driven Pump (EDP) pressure fluctuations:
- Verify system relief valve cracked pressure setting (3000 PSI nominal).
- Check hydraulic return line filter differential pressure indicator.
- Replace main hydraulic return filter element if pop-out indicator is tripped.
- Perform case drain flow check on engine-driven pump to determine internal pump wear.
