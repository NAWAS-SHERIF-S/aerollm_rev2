# AIRCRAFT FLIGHT CONTROL SYSTEM MAINTENANCE MANUAL
DOCUMENT TYPE: Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)
SYSTEM: Flight Controls (ATA 27)

SECTION 1: ELEVATOR CONTROL SYSTEM RESISTANCE & LINKAGE INSPECTION
1. If increased resistance or mechanical binding is observed during movement of the elevator control system:
- Inspect elevator control linkages, bellcranks, control cable tension, and pulley assemblies per AMM Chapter 27-30-00.
- Check elevator hydraulic power control unit (PCU) actuator seals, input rod bearings, and servo valve freedom of movement.
- Verify flight control surface hinge pin lubrication condition and check for corrosion or debris at travel stops.
- Perform control system rigging check and cable tension measurement using calibrated tensiometers.
- Conduct full flight control travel range check (full nose-up to full nose-down) and verify cockpit control column deflection.

SECTION 2: FLIGHT CONTROL ACTUATORS & TRIM TAB SYSTEMS
2. Control actuator and elevator trim maintenance:
- Inspect elevator trim tab actuator motor, position transmitter, and mechanical flex drive shafts.
- Perform operational ground test of primary and secondary flight control systems prior to flight authorization.
